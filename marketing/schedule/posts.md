# Solworld: a day of posts

Every post below is a true statement about what the site does. Don't add numbers (buildings sold, holders, volume) unless they're real ones from your site that day.

## How to queue them all at once (about 10 minutes)

1. On x.com, open the post box.
2. Paste the text and attach the photo.
3. Click the **calendar-with-clock icon** under the text.
4. Pick the date and time from the table, then **Confirm → Schedule**.
5. Repeat for each post. See or edit the queue under **Drafts → Scheduled**.

Times are New York time. Change the start to whatever suits you; just keep the 2-hour gaps.
