### Tips
- **Post when it's night in New York** (around 9 pm–1 am ET). That's when the lit-window screenshots look best.
- **Pin Day 1.** Quote-tweet it every day with that day's feature.
- **Use real numbers** from your Operator tools (buildings owned, landowners, volume). Don't make up stats.
- **Keep it about the game.** Buildings are virtual collectibles, not real property. Avoid promising profits.
